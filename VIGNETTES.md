## Vignette 1: The "Ameno Plan" (DARVO & Dysregulation Cycle)

**Clinical Context:** 
- **Sebastián:** 49 years old, lawyer, has children from a previous marriage. Exhibits a predominantly secure/stable attachment style.
- **Mariela:** 42 years old, lawyer, no children. Exhibits a severe father-abandonment trauma schema, supported by an extended family system (clan) that actively reinforces her dysregulation and victim-narratives.
- **Current situation:** Mariela is having an emotionally difficult day. Sebastián attempts to set a boundary regarding previous aggressive behavior, triggering a massive cycle of DARVO (Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender).
