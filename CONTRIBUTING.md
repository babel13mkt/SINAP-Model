## 🤝 How to Contribute

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. **Create** a feature branch: `git checkout -b feature/your-feature-name`
3. **Commit** your changes with a descriptive message.
4. **Push** to your fork and open a **Pull Request**.

### Contribution Areas

- 🧮 **Model Extensions**: Propose new variables or sub-models (e.g., group dynamics, longitudinal tracking).
- 🐍 **Code Improvements**: Optimize `sinap_calculator.py`, add unit tests, or create visualization tools.
- 📚 **Documentation**: Improve variable definitions, add clinical case examples, or translate documents.
- 🔬 **Empirical Validation**: Propose study designs to validate model predictions against clinical data.

---

## ⚠️ Ethical Guidelines

- All clinical case examples submitted must be **fully anonymized**.
- Do not submit data that could identify real individuals.
- The SINAP model is a heuristic tool and must **not** be presented as a diagnostic instrument.
